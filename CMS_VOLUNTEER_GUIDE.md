# 📖 Website CMS Guide for Volunteers

## Welcome! 👋

This guide will help you easily update the church website without any technical knowledge. The Content Management System (CMS) is designed to be volunteer-friendly with clear labels and helpful descriptions.

## 🔐 Accessing the CMS

1. Go to your website at: `yourdomain.com/admin`
2. Log in with your Sanity credentials
3. You'll see a friendly dashboard with emoji icons and clear section names

## 📋 What You Can Edit

The left sidebar shows all the sections you can edit, organized with clear emoji icons:

### 🏠 **Home & Hero Page**
- Update the main homepage
- Change the rotating headline words
- Upload new background photos for the slideshow
- Edit service times and welcome messages
- Manage FAQ section

### ⛪ **Church Info & Logo**
- Update church name, address, phone
- Change the church logo
- Update social media links

### 🕊️ **Pastor & History**
- Update pastor information
- Edit church history and story

### 👥 **Leadership & Board**
- Add or edit board members

- Upload new headshot photos (drag & drop!)
- Change roles and positions

### 🏫 **Sunday School & Teachers**
- Manage Sunday School staff
- Update class assignments
- Upload teacher photos
- Edit schedule information

### 📖 **Statement of Faith & Beliefs**
- Update church beliefs and doctrine
- Edit theological statements

### ✨ **Mission, Vision & Story**
- Update mission statement

### 🗓️ **Weekly Services Schedule**

- Manage page redirects (Advanced)

## 🎯 How to Update Content

### Adding a New Person (Board Member, Teacher, etc.)

1. Click on the section (e.g., "👥 Leadership & Board")
2. Scroll to the appropriate list (Board Members or Ministry Coordinators)
3. Click the **"Add item"** button
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **👤 Full Name**: Type the person's name
   - **💼 Position/Title**: Type their role
   - **📷 Profile Photo**: 
     - Click the photo area
     - Drag and drop an image OR click "Upload" to browse
     - The photo will automatically upload to the website
   - **🎯 Photo Position**: Leave this blank unless the photo needs adjustment
5. Click **"Publish"** at the bottom to save your changes

### Editing Existing Content

1. Click on the section you want to edit
2. Find the field you want to change
3. Type your new text directly in the field
4. Click **"Publish"** at the bottom to save

### Uploading Photos

The CMS now accepts image uploads directly! You no longer need to manually place files.

1. Click on any **📷 Profile Photo** field
2. You can either:
   - **Drag and drop** your image directly onto the field
   - Click **"Upload"** to browse and select from your computer
3. The image will be automatically uploaded and optimized
4. Click **"Publish"** to save

## ✅ Important Tips

### Before You Publish
- ✓ Double-check spelling and punctuation
- ✓ Make sure photos are clear and well-lit
- ✓ Verify phone numbers and email addresses
- ✓ Preview your changes if possible

### Photo Guidelines
- Use **portrait-oriented headshots** for people
- Photos should be **well-lit** and **in focus**
- **Background should be simple** (solid color is best)
- File size: Keep under 10MB per photo
- Formats: JPG, PNG, or WEBP work best

### Getting Help
- Each field has a **description** below it explaining what to enter
- Look for **placeholder text** (gray text showing examples)
- **Required fields** will show an error if left empty
- If you make a mistake, just edit and republish!

## 🚨 Common Questions

**Q: I made a change but don't see it on the website**
- Make sure you clicked the **"Publish"** button at the bottom
- Wait 1-2 minutes for changes to go live
- Try refreshing your browser (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)

**Q: Can I undo a change?**
- Yes! Sanity CMS keeps a history of all changes
- Ask your website administrator to help restore a previous version

**Q: What if I delete something by accident?**
- Don't panic! Your admin can recover deleted content
- Contact your website administrator right away

**Q: Can multiple people edit at the same time?**
- Yes! The CMS supports multiple editors
- You'll see a notification if someone else is editing the same section

**Q: How do I know my changes are saved?**
- Look for a green success message after clicking "Publish"
- The "Publish" button will turn green when there are no unsaved changes

## 📞 Need Help?

If you run into any issues or have questions:
1. Take a screenshot of what you're seeing
2. Note what you were trying to do
3. Contact your website administrator
